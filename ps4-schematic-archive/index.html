Consoles CUH-20xx / 21xx / 22xx AC 100–240 V, 2.5 A DC +12 V 13 A / +4.8 V 1.5 A Topology PFC + LLC + SR + flyback standby Source 2 pages, 523 KB PDF
Read this before probing anything. The primary side is mains-live, and C114 / C19 hold more than 380 V for minutes after unplug. Discharge them and verify with a meter first. Never ground a scope on the primary without an isolation transformer and a differential probe. The +12 V / +4.8 V secondary is SELV and safe to probe normally.
